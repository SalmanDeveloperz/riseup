# RiseUp Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

RiseUp is an alarm clock app. We built it so that **your data never leaves your iPhone**.

## What RiseUp stores (only on your iPhone)
- **Your alarms**: times, repeat days, labels, sounds and missions.
- **Your wake-up history**: when alarms rang, when you got up, snoozes, mission time and the mood you picked.
- **Photo missions**: when you register a spot, RiseUp keeps a small thumbnail and a mathematical "fingerprint"
  of the photo to recognise the spot later. The full photo is not saved. Morning photos are compared and then discarded.
- **Barcode missions**: the text of the code you registered.
- **Your own alarm sounds**: a 30-second copy of the sound you picked from Files or of a video's sound. Only the
  file you pick is read; RiseUp sees nothing else in Files or Photos.
- **Settings**: such as your default sound, the bedtime reminder time and your last sleep-sound mix.

## What RiseUp does NOT do
- No account and no sign-in.
- No servers: RiseUp does not send your data anywhere.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no data sold or shared with anyone.

## Permissions and why
| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Alarms | Scheduling your alarms so they ring even when the app is closed |
| Camera | Photo and Barcode missions (only while the mission screen is open) |
| Motion & Fitness | Steps mission (counting steps) |
| Notifications | The optional bedtime reminder |

You can change these at any time in the iPhone's Settings app → RiseUp.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the RiseUp app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

## Children
RiseUp does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
